# CarCare Fleet: approved claims and proposed landing-page copy

Use alongside `CURSOR_PROMPT.md`. **CarCare Fleet** is a business-facing name for the **CarCare Diary** app. No team collaboration, GPS tracking or vehicle telematics. Avoid claims of guaranteed downtime reduction. Product claim source: owner-confirmed features and supplied app screenshots.

## Shared truth / footer & pricing
- Fleet supports many vehicles; do not frame as *only* for small businesses or imply a fixed vehicle ceiling.
- Manual and bulk odometer updates; service/repair logs and histories; reminders and upcoming/overdue tasks; inspection checklists; spending and fuel-category expense logs; fleet-level analytics; documents; expense-data exports (screens show CSV/PDF).
- Free tier: track **one** vehicle, create service logs and receive due reminders. **One-month free trial available.** Do not show fixed subscription prices. Do not promise a credit-card-free trial or that *all* fleet features remain free.
- This is an **app**, available through the existing App Store / Google Play links, not a GPS fleet tracking system or a multi-user fleet management platform.
